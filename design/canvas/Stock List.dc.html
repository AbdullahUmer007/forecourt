2a Mobile · light · filter collapsed

The whole facet panel is one <details>: 52px closed, showing the applied count. Tap it open and each facet group is its own nested details. Works with scripting off, and keyboard-operable for free.

2b Mobile · light · filter open

Zero-match options stay in place, greyed, with a 0 count and pointer-events:none — the list never reshuffles under your thumb.

2c Mobile · dark · zero results

Three moves, in order: say plainly what we do not have · show the four closest real cars with what we widened · offer the alert with an unticked consent box and the unsubscribe promise in the wording.

2d Desktop · light · grid, 118 in stock
2e Desktop · dark · list view

List view earns its place with no JS because it is a different URL, not a different stylesheet: ?view=list renders denser markup with a 220px photograph, four spec columns and the price right-aligned — the shape a buyer comparing 118 cars on a laptop actually wants.

2f Desktop · light · only 12 cars in stock

A twelve-car dealer gets no pagination, no filter chips, and a closing panel that turns a short list into a deliberate one — "that is all 12, you have seen the whole forecourt" — plus four proof figures where a 118-car dealer would have page links.
