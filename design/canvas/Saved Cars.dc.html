Saved cars

Anonymous by default, a comparison tool rather than a bookmarks folder. Saving is a form POST, comparing is a URL, and a sold car keeps its place on the list with the closest live alternative attached. Scroll inside each frame.

turn 5 · 5a–5g

5a Mobile · light · 3 saved

Each row carries the six figures a buyer actually compares — price, monthly payment, year, mileage, MOT expiry, declared marks — so the list is already half a comparison before you press the button.

5b Mobile · dark · comparison

On a phone the table scrolls sideways with the row labels pinned by position:sticky. Three columns is the cap — a fourth makes every figure too narrow to read.

5c Mobile · light · one has sold

The sold car keeps its figures, drops to 45% photo opacity and ink-subtle titling, loses its checkbox and its Remove button, and gains a named alternative with the three reasons it is close — price, mileage, shape.

5d Desktop · light · list
5e Desktop · dark · comparison, with a sold column
5f Empty state · light and dark

The empty state teaches the mechanism ("tap the heart"), states the payoff ("save two or three and this page lays them side by side"), and gives six real entry points with live counts. The saved-searches panel stays visible, so a buyer with nothing saved can still leave with a watched search — which is the lead the dealer actually wants.
