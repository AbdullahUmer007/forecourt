Finance promotion

One component, four states. The mandated eight items appear in CONC 3.5.5R order in every state that shows a figure, and the representative APR is the largest thing in the block — bigger than the monthly payment it sits beside.

turn 4 · 4a–4g

4a Standalone block · light and dark · 720

The APR is set at clamp(52px, 7.4cqw, 104px) against the payment's fixed 26px, so prominence is not a judgement call — it is 2:1 at every width this block ships at (52px in a 680px panel and at 375), widening past 4:1 only in containers above about 1400px. It can never invert, because the payment does not scale. Inside the ordered list the APR row repeats that: brand rule, tinted ground, 26px value, bold label, while the other seven rows sit at 14px.

4b Mobile · 375 · light and dark
4c No-JavaScript calculator

Two radios and three selects, one submit, one server round trip. The result band below the form is what you land on after the reload — "Quote updated", the chosen terms restated, the APR at 80px and a freshly computed example. No slider, because a figure that moves as you drag has no example attached to it at the moment you read it.

4d Absent — no approved example exists

Not a single figure — no payment, no rate, no "from" price, not even a struck-through example. The reason is stated as a standard we hold ourselves to rather than a system failure, and the panel on the right promises exactly what the buyer will get in writing.
