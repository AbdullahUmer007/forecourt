# Deal discount approval

Sales staff request approval of the current below-advertised cash price, with a reason. Another permitted staff member reviews and approves or declines within their saved role limit; owner has no cap, non-owner with no configured limit cannot approve. Read role/membership from the database at decision time, apply tenant/branch/ownership permissions, refuse self-approval and stale review. Keep requests and decisions as append-only note evidence and audit. Bind to exact deal revision and advertised price so later edits invalidate approval. Serialize on the deal. Gate discounted cash quotations and the simple deal-to-invoice workflow, without changing stock, deal state, payments or finance. No new VAT calculations, migration or provider calls. Acceptance: permissions/site/tenant/limits/self/stale rejection, duplicate decisions, quote gate, retained reason/history, UI request/review state. Rollback routes/actions and restore below-advertised refusal; retain evidence. Existing generic invoice/lifecycle APIs outside the simplified workflow require separate review.


## Delivered
Per-deal request/review page with advertised price, cash price, exact discount, reason and retained decision history. The requester cannot approve their own request. Decisions check the current active membership, permissions and configured role limit in the database. Requests and decisions append evidence and audit records; concurrent repetitions are idempotent. Approval is invalidated by later deal revision or advertised-price changes. Discount arithmetic reuses the property-tested domain money helpers.

Discounted saved cash quotations and the simple deal-to-invoice flow now require a current approval, with direct links to the approval page. Existing generic invoice/lifecycle APIs remain outside this slice and require a separate end-to-end enforcement review. Full settlement, an approval inbox/notifications and role-limit editing UI remain follow-on work. No financial transaction, invoice issue or Railway deployment.
